📱 How to Test the App

  1. Open the WITTYZ app on your iPhone
  2. Sign in and select a quiz
  3. Start a game
  4. Display this page on a second screen (computer, tablet, or another phone)
  5. Select the question number below that matches your current question
  6. Point your iPhone camera at the colored QR code matching your answer choice
  7. The app will scan the code and advance to the next question
Red = Answer 1
Blue = Answer 2
Green = Answer 3
Yellow = Answer 4
